Mastery looks like this: Before you write a word, you upload your professor's rubric to ChatGPT. You ask "what does this professor prioritize in grading? Then you use that analysis to structure your research. You ask ChatGPT for a research map: what are the major perspectives on this topic? What are the key debates? What search terms should I use in academic databases? You take those terms to JSTOR and Google Scholar and find real sources.

Then you write your outline and ask for feedback against the rubric priorities—before you've written a word of your actual paper.
