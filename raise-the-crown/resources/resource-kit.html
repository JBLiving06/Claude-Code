Tool Recommendation Guide

Not all AI tools are created equal. Here's what actually works, organized by use case.

💬 General AI Assistants

ChatGPT (OpenAI)
Free $20/mo Pro
The most versatile AI assistant. GPT-4o mini (free tier) handles most tasks. GPT-4o (paid) excels at complex reasoning and long documents.
Free: GPT-4o mini, unlimited | Plus: $20/month for GPT-4o with higher limits
Best for: Structured workflows, exam prep, rubric engineering. The free tier is genuinely powerful.
Claude (Anthropic)
Free $20/mo Pro
Excels at nuanced reasoning, creative writing, and complex analysis. Better at following detailed instructions than ChatGPT.
Free: Claude 3.5 Haiku, limited messages | Pro: $20/month for Claude 3.5 Sonnet with extended limits
Best for: Research synthesis, essay outlining, philosophical reasoning. Pick this if you think in paragraphs, not bullet points.
Google Gemini
Free Student Pricing
Deeply integrated with Google Workspace. Strong at multimodal tasks (text, images, video). Free tier is generous.
Free: Gemini 2.0 Flash, unlimited | AI Premium: Reduced student pricing for Gemini 2.0 Pro
Best for: If you live in Google Docs/Sheets/Slides. Underrated for academic research with Drive integration.
Microsoft Copilot
Free
Built into Windows, Edge, and Microsoft 365. Powered by GPT-4 technology with web search integration.
Free: GPT-4 access with web search | Pro: $20/month for priority access
Best for: Windows users who want GPT-4 quality without paying. Solid web search capabilities.

🔬 Research & Citations

Perplexity
Free Student Discount
AI-powered search engine with inline citations. Best for research questions that need current, sourced information.
Free: 5 Pro searches/day | Pro: $20/month (student discount available for $10/month)
Game-changer for literature reviews and research synthesis. The citations are actually accurate.
Elicit
Free
AI research assistant for academic papers. Searches across 125+ million papers, summarizes findings, extracts data.
Free: 5,000 one-time credits | Plus: $10/month for researchers
Essential for STEM research. Saves hours on systematic reviews and meta-analyses.
Semantic Scholar
100% Free
Free academic search engine by the Allen Institute for AI. Automatically extracts key figures, citations, and influences.
Free: Unlimited access to 200+ million papers
Best free alternative to Google Scholar. Superior citation graphs and paper recommendations.
Google NotebookLM
100% Free
Upload your course readings, papers, or notes. Ask questions and get answers grounded only in your sources.
Free: Unlimited notebooks and sources
Criminally underrated. Perfect for synthesizing lecture notes and course readings before exams.

✍️ Writing & Editing

Grammarly
Free Student Discount
Real-time grammar, clarity, and tone suggestions. Works everywhere you write (email, docs, social media).
Free: Basic grammar and spelling | Premium: $12/month for students (regularly $30/month)
The standard. Premium is worth it if you write a lot. Free tier catches 90% of issues.
QuillBot
Free
Paraphrasing tool, grammar checker, and summarizer. Helps rewrite dense academic language into clarity.
Free: 125 words per paraphrase | Premium: $9.95/month for unlimited
Great for breaking down complex sources and improving sentence flow. Use ethically.
Hemingway Editor
Free Web
Highlights complex sentences, passive voice, and readability issues. Makes your writing bold and clear.
Free: Web version | Desktop: $19.99 one-time purchase
Simple, focused, effective. Run your essays through this before submitting.

💻 Coding & Development

GitHub Copilot
FREE for Students
AI pair programmer that suggests code completions in real-time. Works in VS Code, JetBrains, and more.
Students: 100% free with GitHub Education | Pro: $10/month for non-students
No-brainer for CS students. Sign up for GitHub Education immediately if you haven't.
Cursor
Free $20/mo Pro
AI-first code editor built on VS Code. Chat with your codebase, generate entire features with natural language.
Free: 2,000 completions/month | Pro: $20/month for unlimited
Next-generation coding experience. More powerful than Copilot for large refactors and new features.
Replit
Free Student Plans
Browser-based IDE with AI assistance. Code, collaborate, and deploy without setup. Perfect for quick prototypes.
Free: Public repls with AI assistance | Core: $25/month for private projects + more AI
Best for learning to code or building quick demos. Zero setup friction.

🎨 Presentations & Design

Canva
Free FREE Pro for Students
Drag-and-drop design tool for presentations, posters, social media graphics. AI image generation and text-to-design.
Free: 250,000+ templates | Students: Free Pro with .edu email (normally $15/month)

Gamma
Free
AI-powered presentation builder. Describe your talk, Gamma generates a full slide deck with design and content.
Free: 400 AI credits | Plus: $10/month for unlimited
Fastest way to go from outline to polished presentation. Great for conference talks and class presentations.

Free Trial
Smart presentation software with adaptive templates. Automatically adjusts layouts as you add content.
Free: Limited presentations | Pro: $12/month for teams

Tiered Investment Framework

You don't need to spend $100/month to be competitive. Here's how to think about AI tool investment based on your budget and needs.

The Foundation
$0/month
"This is not the struggle tier."
  • ChatGPT Free (GPT-4o mini)
  • Google Gemini Free
  • Microsoft Copilot Free
  • Perplexity Free (5 Pro searches/day)
  • Elicit Free
  • Google NotebookLM
  • GitHub Copilot (free for students)
  • Semantic Scholar
These free tools are genuinely powerful. You can complete 90% of academic tasks at this tier. The limitations are usage caps, not capability.
The Sweet Spot
$20-30/month
"One subscription transforms everything."
  • Pick ONE: ChatGPT Plus ($20) OR Claude Pro ($20)
  • Perplexity Pro ($20/mo, or $10/mo student)
  • Everything from The Foundation tier
You don't need both ChatGPT and Claude. Pick the one that fits how you think. ChatGPT for structured workflows, Claude for nuanced reasoning. Add Perplexity Pro if research is central to your work.
The Builder
$50-75/month
"Building systems, not just asking questions."
  • ChatGPT Plus ($20)
  • Claude Pro ($20)
  • Perplexity Pro ($10-20)
  • Notion AI ($10 add-on)
  • Cursor Pro ($20) OR Grammarly Premium ($12)
For students who are building products, managing complex research projects, or creating content systems. You're not just consuming AI—you're architecting workflows.
Full Stack
$100+/month
"Professional-grade toolkit."
  • Everything from The Builder tier
  • Google AI Premium
  • Cursor Pro
  • Grammarly Premium
  • Gamma Plus
  • Specialty tools for your field
Most students don't need this yet. But know it exists for when you're working professionally, building startups, or doing intensive research. This is the creator/entrepreneur tier.

Protocol-to-Tool Mapping

The four protocols from the workshop work best with specific tools. Here's how to match your workflow to the right technology.

Rubric Engineering
Reverse-engineering assignment expectations into AI-generated scoring frameworks.
→
Best: ChatGPT Plus or Claude Pro — both excel at structured, instruction-following tasks
→
Free alternative: ChatGPT Free (GPT-4o mini) — surprisingly strong at rubric generation
→
Why these: Need consistent formatting, attention to detail, ability to iterate on complex criteria
Research Synthesis
Using AI to map academic conversations, identify key debates, and synthesize literature.
→
Research phase: Perplexity Pro + Elicit + Semantic Scholar — find and map the literature
→
Synthesis phase: Claude Pro or Google NotebookLM — analyze patterns and generate insights
→
Free alternative: Google NotebookLM + Semantic Scholar — surprisingly powerful combination
→
Why these: Need citation accuracy, ability to synthesize contradictory sources, nuanced reasoning
Pre-Class Prep
Transforming course readings into understanding through strategic AI-assisted pre-reading.
→
Best: Google NotebookLM — upload readings, ask clarifying questions, generate study guides
→
Alternative: ChatGPT or Claude with uploaded PDFs — works for shorter readings
→
Recording lectures: Otter.ai — transcribe and search lectures later
→
Why these: Need source-grounded responses, ability to handle long documents, fast Q&A
Exam Systems
Building AI-powered study systems that generate practice questions and custom study materials.
→
Best: ChatGPT Plus — strongest at generating structured practice exams and flashcards
→
Alternative: Claude Pro — better for essay-based exams and conceptual questions
→
Study guide creation: NotebookLM + ChatGPT/Claude — upload notes, generate comprehensive guides
→
Why these: Need consistent output formats, ability to generate varied question types, iterative refinement
